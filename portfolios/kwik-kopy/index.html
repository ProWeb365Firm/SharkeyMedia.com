KWIK KOPY

Printing’s new place online

Full of colour, custom features, and pages upon pages of fresh content, the website we created for Kwik Kopy is both fun and functional. An exciting web design with innovative web development work allows the high-quality customer service – for which the long-standing print and design company is known – take centre stage.

Integrated with Sharkey Media’s own Canvas CMS, content on the website can be easily updated and added to by the Kwik Kopy team. An online request-a-quote system, handy centre locator and an info-packed Resource Centre are all included in the site, along with detailed information on all of Kwik Kopy’s products and services.
